I work on efficient, hardware-aware machine learning: making models cheaper to run, and using ML to make hardware faster to design. My current work builds graph neural network surrogates that predict how a neural network will perform on an AI accelerator, so that large design spaces can be searched without running a full simulation for every point. I also study what quantization actually does to large models, beyond average benchmark scores.

I am looking for a PhD position starting Fall 2027 in AI hardware, ML systems, or efficient ML. Feel free to reach out by email.

Research interests

  • Efficient and hardware-aware ML; algorithm–hardware co-design for edge AI inference
  • ML-guided design space exploration, performance modeling, and mapping of tensor workloads on AI accelerators
  • Efficient adaptation and inference of large models: parameter-efficient fine-tuning and quantization
  • Digital and VLSI design for edge AI hardware
